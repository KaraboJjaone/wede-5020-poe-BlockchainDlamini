Welcome to the South African Business Council NPC, where we are passionately dedicated to making a tangible and lasting impact on the lives of individuals and communities in South Africa.

Our focus

Employment creation, safer communities, period poverty support, and a sustainable business ecosystem for those in need.

About us

Driven by people, purpose, and practical support

As a non-profit organisation, we focus on critical issues requiring urgent attention. Established in 2019, we are proudly a level one, black-owned, female-operated organisation delivering impactful solutions.

We love what we do and are passionate about making a difference. We believe in people more than business plans, and we are open to engaging and offering turnkey solutions.

Our Mission

Create employment, reduce gender based violent crimes, eliminate period poverty, and create a sustainable business ecosystem for all those in need.

Our Vision

Make positive change within the South African business sector, create wealth, reduce poverty and crime, and be remembered as a trusted partner.
