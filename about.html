About Us

Welcome to the South African Business Council NPC, where we are passionately dedicated to making a tangible and lasting impact on the lives of individuals and communities in South Africa. Our mission is to tackle unemployment, gender-based violence, and period poverty while supporting disadvantaged groups including women, youth, and schoolgirls.

As a non-profit organisation, we focus on critical issues requiring urgent attention. Established in 2019, we are proudly a level one, black-owned, female-operated organisation delivering impactful solutions.

We love what we do and are passionate about making a difference.

We believe in people more than business plans.

We are open to engaging and offer turnkey solutions.

Our Mission

Create employment, reduce gender based violent crimes, eliminate period poverty and to create a sustainable business ecosystem for all those that are in need.

Our Vision

Make positive change within the South African business sector. Create wealth, reduce poverty and crime. To be remembered as a trusted, reliable and reputable partner.
